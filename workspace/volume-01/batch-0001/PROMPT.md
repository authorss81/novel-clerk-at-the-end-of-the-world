## Rules That Govern This Batch

These are not suggestions. Violating one breaks the volume.

- **Owen is Stage 0, a Copy Clerk, for the whole batch.** He signs no continuation, no certification, and no hold. His only signature is his own statement of his part in the Chapter 6 notice, which is adverse to him and needs no qualified signer. His **first qualified filing as an assisting clerk is Batch 0002 (Chapters 11–20)** and must not appear here.
- **The transfer's receipt is validly made and cannot be corrected.** Tal Sarr signed it while alive, late in C.Y. 486, and the Office received it then. He drowned in the C.Y. 487 spring flood, months later, and his death disturbed nothing. It is never a forgery, never a posthumous filing, and never a lie.
- **The only provable defect in the file is the annex schedule's two-day backdating.** Owen proves it in Chapter 1 and deliberately withdraws it from his own inquiry in Chapter 5. It is never corrected. Three requests refused inside that two-day window stay logged and stay unexplained in this batch.
- **Eleven days is the consolidation letter and the families at Arbour Kitchen. Eleven months is the Sarr receipt.** Do not blend them.
- **Four instruments stay apart:** the pre-existing spring engineering notice on the Ninth Weir; the transfer receipt; Pike's Chapter 8 load-restriction suspension, the first instrument in the case with a real appeal window; and **Stability Order 114**, which is not issued until Chapters 17–18, on the eleven failing returns named in Chapter 3. Never conflate the Chapter 8 suspension with Stability Order 114.
- **Three separate seven-day clocks:** the return's own review interval, the answer clock on the Chapter 9 petition that runs to a written answer in Chapters 12–13, and the closure interval. One never satisfies another.
- **Lower Lock's arithmetic:** thirteen households on the last accepted roll; nine ratify in Chapter 6 and become the nine adult branches of the draft return; four vote no, sign no branch in this batch, and are offered a separate branch in Chapters 31–40. Ada is one of the four. The three households that demand their own pages in Chapter 7 are inside the nine.
- **The thirteen erased entries** are six children or people unable to answer and seven adults who can answer. Ivy Sarr, 12, is in the six. Dory Anstruther, 19, is in the seven. **Cobb Rennie is not one of the thirteen** and is not one of the thirteen households; his two-year erasure is older than this case and nothing filed here reaches him.
- **Two living observers with separable interests** clear the threshold on the return, and they are **Junia Pell** and **Wend Voss**. Both witnessed acts only, neither touched the drafting, and Pell's independence is contestable on the record because she is a Registry officer. Never write a single witness on this return.
- **A household return is a set of separately consenting adult branches.** No adult signs for another. One household's withdrawal narrows the return; it does not destroy it.
- **A claim to a status is not a claim to a duty.** The return claims that the ward *performs maintenance of a named public crossing*, not that the crossing is open, safe, funded, or usable. Teach the difference; do not let Owen stumble into it.
- **A suspension preserves a status or an appeal window only.** It never makes the Ninth Weir safe, funded, repaired, or open to loads. The crossing stays closed to loads for the entire volume.
- **The instrument is a district return**, matching its final file number 487-LL. "Local return" is the general category word for any community-filed return, never a second instrument for this filing. An official circular may use the category word in its own language.
- **Marginalia is sparse, plain, and rare.** At most one margin notice in a chapter, and only when Owen is personally comparing a genuine record with the physical act in front of him. Thin and contradictory files answer him. Closed, held, and sealed files return silence, and the Chapter 7 and Chapter 10 silences are that silence. No numbers, no bars, no skill lists, no solution buttons, no software jargon.
- **Form Zero appears once, in Chapter 10, as a clerk's aside:** the Common Return is the only instrument that could supersede a stability order, it has no valid signature path, and it is due in thirteen years. Nothing more is revealed.
- **Verity Lound is offstage.** Her first circular arrives sealed at the end of Chapter 9 and is read in Chapter 10; it carries the Council's seal and her countersignature, so the reader learns a Chancellor issues instruments and nothing else. Her second circular comes in Chapters 33–35. She is never on stage in Volume 01.
- **Not on stage in this volume at all:** Warden Kade, the Census Guard, Iven Sallow, Bettis Marr, Tovan Reed, Ansel Rive in person, the Claim Market, the Free Counters, the First Seal Subvault. The Upland fringe and the Claim Market in Chapter 4 are Nell's speech and memory only.
- **The eight-person infancy sponsorship is not referenced, hinted at, or foreshadowed** anywhere in this batch.
- **Nothing restores the dead.** Marta Rusk and Tal Sarr stay dead, and the correct record says so.
- **No new final enemy, no cosmic threat, no simulation, no players, no game designer.**

## The Ten Commitments

1. Lower Lock is a living ward of about 240 residents, not a slum used for suffering. Its people do the work.
2. The omission is real, deliberate, and larger than a clerical error — and the entry that made it is *correct*.
3. Owen's own precision authors the trap in Chapter 6, and being right is what makes him dangerous.
4. The batch reclassifies from **error** to **scope** in Chapter 5, and Owen surrenders the one defect he can prove in order to keep the harder charge alive.
5. The route forward is a **district return the ward files for itself**, and the tool arrives from Nell in Chapter 4, who refuses to be his witness, his advocate, or his relief.
6. Owen's shortcut is refused by Pell in Chapter 9; the lawful foothold is won by Marguerite's petition.
7. Owen ends the batch off the copying floors, with a public statement against himself, and no authority.
8. The answer clock runs to an **answer**, not a hearing, and lands in Batch 0002.
9. The batch ends on consequence and a hook: the circular, the six names needing protective returns, the near-miss with a name, and the brass tab reading **ALDER ROW — 461**.
10. The batch leaves the ward worse-off in material ways and more capable in one. It must not read as a victory.

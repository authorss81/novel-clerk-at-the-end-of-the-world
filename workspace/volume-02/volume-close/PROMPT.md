# Volume 02 — Volume-Close Audit Prompt

Phase: **volume-close for Volume 02, *Clean Count*, Chapters 51–100.** This is not a writing phase unless the audit finds a defect that can only be fixed in prose, and then the fix must be surgical: no chapter is restarted, no scene is rewritten, no planned event is moved.

**Volume 02 is written through. Chapters 51–100 exist as finished prose in `chapters/volume-02/`. The last batch, 91–100, was just written and reviewed. Nothing in this phase may write a Batch 0006 and nothing in it may begin Volume 03.**

## What this phase is for

Volume 01 had a volume-close pass. This is the same pass for Volume 02, and it does five things:

1. **Audits Chapters 51–100 as a whole** for canon conflicts, broken numbers, calendar drift, merged sets, guardrail breaches, duplicated beats, mechanical tics, and prose that has stopped being a scene.
2. **Reconciles the five state files** — `state/current.md`, `state/batch-summaries.md`, `state/chapter-summaries.md`, `state/continuity.md`, `state/open-threads.md`, `state/character-state.md` — **against the prose as it stands**, and records every proposed canon change rather than absorbing it silently.
3. **Writes the volume's close record**: a `VOLUME 02 — CLOSED` section in `state/continuity.md` and a matching close section in `state/open-threads.md` and `state/chapter-summaries.md`, so that Volume 03's first prompt has one place to read.
4. **Decides, explicitly and in writing, what Volume 03 inherits** and what Volume 02 closed, and flags anything that is about to be inherited by accident.
5. **Records the volume's running length** and the register rules the next volume inherits.

## The audit, in the order it must be done

### 1. The calendar, end to end

**Every named weekday, day-of-month, hour of day and elapsed interval in all fifty chapters.** The volume runs from the last days of the Concord Year 487 to the thirtieth day of the fourth month of the Concord Year 488, and it crosses three month boundaries, and the guardrail is that **Day 1 of the Concord Year 487 was a Tuesday, the year turned on a Tuesday, the first month of 488 ran to thirty days so its Day 30 was a Wednesday, the second month opened on a Thursday and ran to thirty, the third month opened on a Saturday and ran to thirty, and the fourth month opens on a Monday.** March's table, as written in the state files, is: **1 Sat · 2 Sun · 3 Mon · 4 Tue · 5 Wed · 6 Thu · 7 Fri · 8 Sat · 9 Sun · 10 Mon · 11 Tue · 12 Wed · 13 Thu · 14 Fri · 15 Sat · 16 Sun · 17 Mon · 18 Tue · 19 Wed · 20 Thu · 21 Fri · 22 Sat · 23 Sun · 24 Mon · 25 Tue · 26 Wed · 27 Thu · 28 Fri · 29 Sat · 30 Sun.** April's: **1 Mon · 2 Tue · 3 Wed · 4 Thu · 5 Fri · 6 Sat · 7 Sun · 8 Mon · 9 Tue · 10 Wed · 11 Thu · 12 Fri · 13 Sat · 14 Sun · 15 Mon · 16 Tue · 17 Wed · 18 Thu · 19 Fri · 20 Sat · 21 Sun · 22 Mon · 23 Tue · 24 Wed · 25 Thu · 26 Fri · 27 Sat · 28 Sun · 29 Mon · 30 Tue.** **Report every mismatch as a blocking finding.** Also verify: the season of 489 opened on **Sunday the second of the third month**, the count of 488 was certified on **Friday the ninth of the second month**, the certification of 489 on **Friday the twenty-eighth of the third month**, and **no chapter may say the season of 489 has a fifth week.**

### 4. Every number, checked

**The volume's ethic is that a figure is checkable, which means the audit must check the figures.** Specifically:

- **Nine thousand four hundred** belongs to the eleven named on the printed schedule of 489 and to those eleven only; Docketford district is over its allocation by the same figure to the unit. **It may not be attached to the ten, to the eleven of any other set, or to the second board, in any chapter of Volume 02.** Count the occurrences and the chapters and report any that is attached to the wrong set.
- **The granary chain.** Counted on the Wednesday night and **ruled on the Thursday**, from the seventh day of the second month of 488: one on the seventh, and the weekly figures thereafter, **the seventh week running as at the Thursday the twenty-seventh of the third month**, and the eighth, ninth, tenth and eleventh on the Thursdays the third, tenth, seventeenth and twenty-fourth of the fourth month. **Check every granary figure in every chapter against that chain and report any that is off by a week.**
- **The ward's week**: marker Thursday and Friday at seven forty; channel Wednesday night; pump prime at four in the morning on the Thursday by **Bram Tillet and a household with a girl of twelve in it** — a household of two in the two rooms in Fetter Lane, and the chapter may not substitute a named twelve-year-old for the household; **roster read in public at ten past six in the evening on the Thursday by Ruth Mapp.**
- **Four thousand one hundred and sixty pounds** is the repair on the crossing at the bottom of Fetter Lane, **belongs to that crossing alone, and is never used as the cost of anything else, and in particular never equated with the cost of ten district surveys.** If any chapter equates them, that is a blocking finding.
- **The crew is nine, the wage is four months and a bit in arrears, and it is the ward crew's wage and may never be migrated to the district office or to a second desk.**
- **The five hours a week** is the cost of the gap in the six protective returns, it is a figure of hours and not a person, and **it is the same figure as the five hours a week of the custody limit at Slackwater and the two are never added together and never called the same five hours.**
- **Stability Order 114**: sixteen named returns, **Lower Lock sixth**, the schedule struck at eight in the morning of the twelfth of the month before last, the order signed on the twenty-fourth at ten past four.
- **Precedent 37/C is two hundred and nine words.** The Office's answer is **two hundred and twenty-four words.** Any chapter that quotes a length quotes the right one.
- **Any other word-count or figure-of-length claimed in the prose**, including the numbers of lines on Pike's sheet, the figures on the survey round, the figures in the fifth column, the figures on the second board, and every word count a character asserts. **Volume 02 has already paid for this class of error three times and it is the one defect class a reader of this book can catch.**

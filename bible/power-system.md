# Power System Bible

## 1. Central Rule

Vespera’s magic is **publicly witnessed fact**. It does not obey commands written on paper. A record gains force only when a real act, a living witness, a clear scope, and continuing support are present.

A clerk’s correction is an institutional way to identify, preserve, contest, and limit that force. The clerk does not create truth from nothing.

## 2. Five Conditions for a Supported Fact

A person, place, relationship, institution, or object can be protected when all five conditions are met:

1. **Named subject:** The filing identifies the subject specifically enough that it cannot secretly cover unlimited people or property.
2. **Performed act:** Something happened, was promised, was maintained, or was deliberately changed.
3. **Independent witness:** At least two living observers with separable interests can attest to what they personally know.
4. **Public scope:** The filing says where the claim applies, such as “the Ninth Weir between Mill Ward and Lower Lock,” not “all future crossings.”
5. **Renewal:** The supporting act is repeated or the filing receives a valid continuation before its deadline.

A document supplies the fifth condition only if it accurately records continuing support. It cannot substitute for the others.

## 3. Everyday Magic

Most citizens can perform small acts of witness without becoming magic users. A midwife, two neighbors, or a household can:

- Confirm that a child has been born and accepted into a chosen family.
- Keep a house’s address present while repairs are under way.
- Certify that a ferry route has been inspected and remains usable.
- Record a death without pretending the dead person can still make new promises.
- Sponsor inheritance for a house, tool, animal, or debt during a settlement period.
- Challenge a false change before a ward meeting.

These are acts of attention, not glowing rituals. The world makes them durable because reciprocal relationships are one of the conditions under which social fact gains unusual weight in Vespera.

### What Ordinary Witnessing Cannot Do

- It cannot create an unbroken bridge from copied engineering notes.
- It cannot make food, tools, medicine, or labor appear.
- It cannot prove that every statement in a document is true.
- It cannot preserve a person after conscious life has completely ended.
- It cannot force a witness to recognize a fact against direct knowledge.
- It cannot let one witness substitute for an entire community when the filing claims to represent that community.

## 4. The Four Parts of a Filing

Every consequential correction has four explicit parts:

### Subject

The exact person, place, relationship, or activity affected. Broad subjects are rejected or narrowed.

### Jurisdiction

The limits of the ruling. Jurisdiction can be geographic, personal, occupational, familial, or procedural. A rule that affects “all people named in Ward Four” may not be used against someone in Ward Five without a separate act.

### Term

The date and hour at which the ruling expires. At that time, the prior status resumes unless another valid filing continues it. A hold never erases its deadline; it makes the deadline visible.

### Effect

The specific status changed, preserved, challenged, or withdrawn. “The bridge is open” is a status. “The bridge is safe” is a judgment about future conditions and requires stronger evidence.

The permanent consequence is the entry left in the public record, including dissent, appeal, and any later correction. Amending the future never erases the old record.

## 5. Deadlines and Relief

- A local continuance is ordinarily reviewed every seven days. A failing return opens a **closure interval** of seven days.
- A person in severe unfiling may receive an emergency suspension for up to 14 days.
- After 14 days without living support, active consciousness becomes unreliable.
- After 30 days, no supported living restoration is possible. The body or an unrecorded trace may continue in some cases, but it cannot be restored as the same recognized person; only a new community of living supporters can establish a new continuity.
- An object can be held at its last supported condition for the same emergency period, but a held crumbling bridge does not become a new bridge.
- A district return can be renewed if a local community assumes a defined duty and the renewal is ratified by the people affected.
- A legal **cache** preserves the last supported record of a district, not a person’s consciousness. Its archive shell may continue to exist beyond a person-specific suspension limit while a living custodian, a current local act, and an appeal route continue to renew the shell. It never preserves consciousness, and any individual branch inside it is not restorable after the 30-day limit; an expired person branch is recorded as lost or unresolved, not carried as a recoverable person. A cache with no such support is an archive shell, not a living suspension.

Deadlines prevent passive safety. A ruling always creates a new obligation before it expires.

## 6. Root Claim

A living person’s **root claim** is their capacity to remain a recognized participant in mutual fact without one authority claiming sole ownership of that participation. It is not visible energy, hit points, or a spendable stat.

A root claim is strengthened by reciprocal acts involving the person: being fed, teaching, trusted, disputed, housed, remembered by another, and participating in duties they have chosen. It weakens through sustained isolation, coercion, or being treated as an object rather than a participant.

Voluntarily sponsoring another supported fact spends part of a root claim. Coerced sponsorship is legally and magically unstable. A coerced seal can preserve an emergency hold, but it cannot renew a claim after the coercion ends.

No interface displays root claim as a number. Characters experience concrete effects such as being absent from a mirror, unable to testify to their own name, or being unable to open a case in their own name.

## 7. Personal, Protective, and Collective Returns

A **personal return** is filed by one person for a defined place, relationship, or one-person branch. A household return is an aggregate of separately consenting adult branches: each adult performs a current act and keeps an individual withdrawal and departure right, and no adult signs for another. A household return needs one independent living witness for each branch it carries, in addition to the filer, so the two-observer threshold is met; one witness never speaks for the whole household. A **refusal return** uses the same threshold and states that the filer declines representation while still accepting a limited set of reciprocal duties.

A **protective return** may be entered for a child or a person unable to communicate only when two unrelated living witnesses, a current care act, and a named local appeal route are present. It preserves the person’s status while the care situation exists; it cannot bind the person’s future consent, labor, identity, vote, or private relationships. A protective return must be reviewed every seven days.

A **Common Table** may include many people and local duties. A member’s withdrawal removes that member’s branch immediately. It does not erase the table if the remaining members can replace the affected duty before the term ends. If they cannot, the table narrows its claim or expires. A community may leave the central aggregate without losing its own local return only if it files a **community refusal return** and continues the local acts named in that return. The central clerk must record the withdrawal rather than treating it as silence. If a community has no current return, refusal, or protective coverage, the aggregate must show a gap rather than fill it by assumption.

### Bounded Completeness

The central clerk cannot prove that every hidden person or mobile community exists. Form Zero therefore uses a bounded, public procedure rather than pretending to be a universal census. The received branch list, the boundary of the network, the public challenge notice, and the unresolved branches are all part of the record. A return may be valid within that boundary only when no proxy is used, every branch included in the aggregate is independently authorized, every other received branch is explicitly unresolved and outside the aggregate, and a live appeal route remains available. An unrecorded or unreachable branch is an explicit cost, never an assumed consent.

## 8. Jurisdiction Failure

A correction may be challenged when its jurisdiction is vague, expired, transferred without consent, or used beyond the witnessed act. A challenge does not erase the original entry.

A deliberately false filing can produce a **counter-entry** when contrary acts reach equal or greater support. Consequences are specific:

- An invented debt can collapse, along with assets built from its lending power.
- A falsified road can vanish from supported travel, trapping people who relied on it.
- A coerced custody claim can be broken, though custody may still have been necessary during its legal term.
- A person who knowingly repeats a false claim can be barred from witness authority.

The world does not spontaneously execute people for narrative convenience. Social and legal consequences must be established by supporters, opponents, institutions, and material conditions.

## 9. Emergencies, Holds, and Counter-Signs

A **suspension** prevents one immediate change for a defined term. It does not solve the cause. A clerk can hold a person’s current name, a bridge’s last supported outline, or a hospital ward’s emergency status, but cannot prevent flood, infection, hunger, or injury by declaration.

A **counter-sign** is the right to challenge an accepted false entry. It requires evidence of a supported contrary fact and does not merely assert that the official is lying.

Suspensions can stabilize one condition, but a cluster of dependent entries may fail together. An entire ward can be safe from legal closure while its waterworks disappear, creating a physical disaster.

## 10. Surrogates and Other Emergent Facts

When an obligation is recorded repeatedly, strongly witnessed, and then abandoned by every living performer, it may assemble into a **surrogate**.

A surrogate is not a ghost or a person’s hidden soul. It is a compulsion made from repeated acts. Examples include:

- A dead ferry company still demanding fares from boats using its dock.
- A demolished boundary stone rebuilding itself around residents who no longer remember why it existed.
- A ward’s old emergency rule ordering every person indoors during a danger that ended generations ago.
- A recorded road repeating the final journey of a bridge crew and carrying travelers into the same closed span.

A surrogate can be dispersed, appealed against, or returned to ordinary law when the underlying practice is renewed, revoked, or supported by a genuinely changed act. Killing a surrogate does not automatically correct the record.

## 11. Owen’s Marginalia

Owen does not have an omnipotent inventory screen. When he is reading a genuine case record and comparing it with the physical act before him, he perceives **marginalia**: pressure in the margins of the page and sparse handwritten notices that identify what the record can currently support.

Marginalia has strict limits:

- It reports a filing’s status, not the hidden truth of the world.
- It can identify a contradiction Owen has already encountered or can directly inspect.
- It cannot reveal sealed information merely because a better clerk would deduce it.
- It can be incomplete when witnesses lie, records are damaged, or jurisdiction is ambiguous.
- It never chooses the morally correct answer.
- It becomes less reliable as Owen mistakes a recorded status for reality.

A typical notice should be rare, brief, and concrete:

> **MARGIN NOTICE**
> Subject: Ninth Weir, public crossing.
> Present support: contested.
> Next act required: inspection by one unaffiliated witness.
> Effect if unrenewed: crossing loses recognized access on the seventh evening.

The prose should not use software jargon, experience bars, skill trees, or automated solution buttons.

## 12. Owen’s Special Disposition

### Common Clerk

A **Common Clerk** is a formally trained custodian who can receive a claim from several independently consenting parties and transmit it as a common case. The title is an administrative qualification, not a rank of personal power. A Common Clerk must still obey the scope and term of every return they receive.

Owen can receive **surplus custody** when several independent parties voluntarily place one unresolved fact under the same temporary case. He can hold the filing open without taking any party’s consent from that party.

This capacity comes from his jointly sponsored infancy. Each sponsor’s continuing act contributes a separate thread to a **distributed root claim**; none of the threads belongs to Owen, and Owen cannot renew a thread on a sponsor’s behalf. A thread is tied to the public duty, not to the sponsor’s biological life: after a sponsor dies or leaves, a new willing custodian may perform the same duty and renew the thread, but may not inherit the sponsor’s identity, consent, or authority. Owen can carry the threads only while their current custodians keep the acts alive and continue to choose the common case. It is not a unique power possessed by no one else; other Common Clerks can hold similar cases, but they have to negotiate each transfer and cannot accept remote, delayed, or hostile sponsorships. Owen is unusually tolerant of a claim split among many unrelated people.

A pooled claim can serve as a **transmission channel** when its members choose to join a case together. It cannot serve as a representative claim for people who did not join. Owen’s special history makes him a possible channel for the Common Return, not a lawful answer for every subject. If he converts the channel into a proxy, the affected return becomes false and the refusal conditions in Form Zero fail.

He cannot:

- Acquire a claim by defeating its holder.
- Sign for a sleeping, dead, absent, or coerced person.
- Make a true act merely by filing a description of it.
- Separate the parts of a fact that truly depend on each other.
- Maintain a common filing after its people stop acting together.

## 13. Progression Stages

Progression comes from case knowledge, voluntary trust, delegation, and the ability to accept limits. It is not improved by killing monsters or collecting forms.

### Stage 0 — Copy Clerk

- **Capability:** Transcribes, compares, notices inconsistencies, and performs ordinary witness acts.
- **Limit:** Cannot change a status; every correction requires a qualified signer and a local act.
- **Cost:** Low magic cost, high social cost. Owen begins believing skill alone should protect him from hierarchy.

### Stage 1 — Sealed Witness

- **Capability:** Certifies one person or small place for a seven-day continuance and perceives basic marginalia.
- **Acquisition:** Completes a qualified filing, accepts a named duty, and learns what the entry cannot support.
- **Cost:** One active hold. Unresolved cases intrude as exact sensory details and forgotten names.

### Stage 2 — Continuance Examiner

- **Capability:** Question parties, inspect contested sites, issue local holds, and reopen ordinary procedural errors.
- **Acquisition:** Passes supervised cases and builds a review record through successful challenges.
- **Cost:** Up to three active holds. Each creates a real responsibility toward the claimant; four or more cause confusion, insomnia, and impaired judgment.

### Stage 3 — Censor of Redactions

- **Capability:** Opposes a sealed erasure order for one named subject and suspends that order for one civic day.
- **Acquisition:** Demonstrates a supported contrary act and accepts responsibility for the suspended risk.
- **Cost:** Spends part of his own root claim. He may temporarily be unable to attest to his own name or access records under his own identity.

### Stage 4 — Case Architect

- **Capability:** Builds a remedy plan from many ordinary acts, delegates portions to qualified people, and can combine compatible local filings into a common case.
- **Acquisition:** Learns coordination without control; must obtain renewed consent after material changes.
- **Cost:** He becomes accountable for the integrity of the combined plan, including deception by a participant. Delay, injury, and resentment can reach him as legal and social consequences.

### Stage 5 — Regional Steward

- **Capability:** Carry a compact mobile filing across regions, inspect sealed records under challenge, and maintain one district’s emergency claim while traveling.
- **Acquisition:** A broad local network consents to hold him responsible, and he accepts at least one unresolved duty he cannot delegate.
- **Cost:** He can be in only one district at a time. The mobile claim weakens if he is absent beyond seven days, forcing him to choose between people rather than solve both remotely.
- **Appointment boundary:** The stage is a qualification and learned capacity. The title **Regional Steward** is a revocable local office; a qualification can remain after Owen loses that office, but it never grants continuing authority over a district.

### Stage 6 — Convene Clerk

- **Capability:** Create and transmit a **Common Table**, a distributed local return that many communities can act on independently while remaining visible in one aggregate case.
- **Acquisition:** Organizes genuine local authority, transparency, withdrawal rights, and review across thousands of claims.
- **Cost:** He has no supreme power over participants. A coerced or false return damages the whole filing, and correction requires reopening the communities rather than overruling them. If he tries to hold mutually incompatible duties in one personal claim, his root claim fragments and he loses the ability to hold several cases until the local claims are separated.

### Stage 7 — Custodian of the Common Return

- **Capability:** Receive every valid local return within the declared bounded network and transmit their combined status as the final filing. Before Civic Zero, he is the only living channel through which the aggregate can be delivered to the central seal. The seal can certify receipt and the boundary of the claim, not consent or universal completeness.
- **Acquisition:** The participating communities choose him despite having every reason not to centralize power. His title is a community-selected transmission role, not an office that owns the people inside the aggregate.
- **Cost:** He can block the form but cannot complete it. Using emergency custody to replace an absent or refusing return creates a false return and causes immediate redactions in the affected region. After a valid activation, the local returns remain the source of authority and his special channel dissolves into ordinary clerk work.

Lound’s authority is separate from Owen’s. Under the Assumption Clause, only the Chancellor holds the institutional power to invoke a proxy line for unfiled districts while the Provisional Mandate is alive. That line is knowingly invalid under Form Zero and can create only a temporary operational hold. Owen can receive and transmit authorized returns; he cannot sign for absent people, and he never inherits Lound’s proxy authority.

Stage 7 is the narrative culmination because Owen’s final restraint is more important than his final spell.

## 14. Failure Rules

- **Bad scope:** Owen attempts to save a whole ward with a filing valid for one household. The filing is narrowed to the household; the ward receives no bonus.
- **Missing material act:** A group repeats a profession of faith, but the pump is still broken. The claim to a working pump fails; a claim to an appeal or repair effort may succeed.
- **Dead sponsor:** An old signature can preserve the past entry but cannot make a new decision.
- **Expired hold:** A temporary correction ends at its term, and the prior state plus current conditions resumes.
- **Coerced return:** A forced local filing is visible but cannot sustain Form Zero. Its invalidity can be challenged by any resident.
- **False continuity:** Repeatedly describing a place as inhabited, without anyone living or maintaining it, may produce a surrogate rather than a home.
- **Counterfeit copy:** A copy without living support is evidence at most. It can be attacked, but it cannot inherit the original’s force.
- **Authority abuse:** Suspending more cases does not increase his rank. It multiplies responsibility and produces physical, social, and legal damage.

## 15. What the System Will Never Automate

The power system must never:

- Tell Owen who is guilty.
- Rewrite history after the fact.
- Restore a fully lost person.
- Turn a confession into objective fact.
- Produce labor, food, tools, medicine, or safe transport.
- Remove a consequence merely because the filing was correct.
- Make unwilling participants willing.
- Turn a successful correction into a free emotional resolution.

The clerk proposes, the community performs, the witnesses attest, and the consequence remains.

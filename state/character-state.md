## Volume 01 — The Unfiled Ward

### Owen Rusk, 32 — Copy Clerk, Fourth River Desk

Wants: Lower Lock kept, and eventually enough legitimate authority to reopen cases the Office has closed. Holds: a clean quarterly run, a sharpened pencil in its case, and no case of his own. Fears: becoming an official whose accuracy decides whose life is documented well enough to continue. Flaw: turns competence into control.

### Nell Arbour, 35 — Claimant Advocate, Arbour Kitchen

Wants: people able to answer for themselves before the rule finishes answering for them. Has not spoken to Owen in two years and will not be his witness, his advocate, or his relief. Holds the finding that ended her Registry career, which Owen copied and filed.

### Ada Rusk, 24 — Bridge Mechanic, Lower Lock

Wants: her ward to have a lawful return of its own. Refuses Owen's money, his plan, and the role of the person he is trying to save. Told him he does not get to be the reason she is safe, and meant it as a fact about filing.

### Junia Pell, 22 — Junior Copy Clerk

Wants: to become competent without becoming a custodian of other people's lives. The first person in the Office to ask who consented. No obligation to Owen and no interest in becoming his instrument.

### Halden Pike, 47 — Deputy Registrar, Docketford

Wants: Docketford solvent, and a decision he can act on by Friday. Believes a stability order is kinder than unmanaged existence. Does not yet know that Owen's name is on a public file adverse to the Office.

### Marguerite Bray, 61 — Ward Steward, Lower Lock

Keeps the ward's paper roll by hand. Uses Owen and refuses to be his. Will file her own petition in her own words when the time comes, and will not thank him for what he proved.

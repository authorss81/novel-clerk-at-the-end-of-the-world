## Where the Batch Starts

It is **batch Day 54, a Saturday**, the seventh week of the rain season, C.Y. 487. Day 1 was a Tuesday, so **Days 45, 52, 59 and 66 are Thursdays** and **Days 49, 56, 63 and 70 are Mondays**, and Day 60 is a Friday. Six things are already running when Chapter 41 opens, and none of them waits:

It is still an order, it still suspends sixteen returns with Lower Lock sixth, and the clerk of the docket said in writing that it is going to be argued **by people and not by a clerk.**
- **The return is renewed for review for the fifth time and the next renewal is due Day 60**, the morning after the hearing. The observers have been re-separated in writing at every renewal and **a separation made once does not carry forward.** The two of them are **Pell**, fourth on a nineteen-name district-exclusion roll she cannot get off without leaving the next instrument with one observer, and **Voss**, a counter clerk in the district that took the crossing away, who has now four written reservations about his own interest in his own hand.

- **The far end of the appeal route is conditionally filled by Hester Lund, who has kept the tide table at Coal Hill for nineteen years**, on seven conditions, of which she withheld the sixth. **The ward's written request went into the ward's bag from the hall door on Day 52 and her answer has not come back.** The standing is **not** a hearing, **not** an appearance and **not** a representative line.

### Chapter 0041 — The Water Comes Up

POV: Owen Rusk, third person limited.

Resistance: there is no villain in this chapter and there must not be one.

### The one thing a writer must not do in Batch 0002

**Do not turn Volume 02 into a campaign.** The count schedule, the reason column, the quota, the fourteen and the thirty are all printed, and they were all printed before the season, and that is the public's answer and it is not a remedy. **A single sheet, a single register, a single clerk's decision or a single successful challenge does not end the refusal-column practice and must not be written as if it did.** If a count correction is overturned in Batch 0002, what changes is one settlement and one person who was one of four, and the practice is still there, and somebody has to say the sentence out loud.

# Canonical Prompt — Volume 01 Close: The Index and Closure Report

**This file is the only place that directs the volume-close pass for Volume 01. It directs an audit, not prose. It is not a batch. It creates no chapters and no further phase.**

Volume 01, *The Unfiled Ward* (Chapters 1–50, C.Y. 487) is finished. Its ten chapters are on disk as finished prose. This phase exists because a finished volume must be indexed and its obligations made explicit before Volume 02 is allowed to touch it, and because the cost of a volume like this one is almost entirely in what it *deliberately* did not do.

## What This Phase Is

**A single agent, one pass, no new prose.** It reads Chapters 1–50 and produces, in `state/`, one document: a **Volume 01 index and closure report**. It also performs the specific consistency work described below.

It must **not**:

- write, rewrite, extend, or "improve" any chapter;
- write a single line of fiction, including a replacement last line for Chapter 50;
- create any `workspace/` directory, any phase prompt, any batch outline, or any `.done` marker;
- write into `reviews/`;
- edit `scripts/`, `.github/workflows/`, `.opencode/agent/`, `AGENTS.md`, `PHASE_SYSTEM.md`, `REPO_BLANK.md`/`REPO_PLAN.md`, `OUTLINE_GUIDE.md`, `opencode.json`, or `state/phase-ledger.json`;
- introduce, hint at, or foreshadow anything that belongs to Volume 02 or later.

## What `state/volume-01-index.md` Must Contain

### 1. A chapter-by-chapter map

For **every one of the fifty chapters**: number, title, the day or days of story time, the weekday, the location, the pressure type, and — the part that matters —

- **the hook it opened**, and
- **where that hook landed**, by chapter number, or **whether it was left open and why**.

A hook that is opened and never mentioned again is the single most expensive defect in a serial of this shape, and Volume 01 has several that were opened on purpose. The report must distinguish three states honestly: **paid**, **left open deliberately**, and **opened and forgotten** — and the third category must be empty, or the report must name what is going to be done about it.

### 2. The full ledger of debts

Two lists, both complete.

- **Debts Volume 01 closed** — with the chapter and the line that closed each one, and a note on what closed it (a person, an act, a document, an instrument, or nothing at all).
- **Debts Volume 01 deliberately left open** — with the chapter that established each one, the reason it was not closed, and the note that the reason is a *choice* and not an oversight. This list is the volume's real subject and it must be long, specific, and free of soft language. If a debt is described as "for now" it has been mislabelled; say instead whose decision it was.

### 3. Every fixed number later prose may not contradict

Pulled out of the prose, not out of the outlines, and each with its chapter. At minimum the report must carry and lock:

- **the scope clause: four words, `Duty, and no status`**, the ward's own, Chapter 46;
- **twenty-one living witnesses, and not one of them Owen Rusk**, with the eight/two/four/six/one arithmetic and the three deliberate absences (Cobb Rennie, Ivy Sarr, Hester Lund) and the reason for each;
- **the renewals: six, and no seventh** — Days 25, 32, 39, 46, 53, 60, all for review and never binding, interval seven days;
- **the certification: sealed Day 63, seven days, expiring at the hour of the seal on Day 70** — and that the volume ends with roughly half an hour of it left;
- **the hold: one active hold, one thing only**, and what it does *not* do;
- the Day 14 roll entry, unamended; the Day 10 load restriction; the six instruments and the rule that they stay apart; the two hundred and twenty-four words; the one hundred and nine entries and the forty-one dispositions; the one hundred and eighty-one and the twenty-two; the four thousand one hundred and sixty; four months of arrears; the five hours a week; **eleven days and eight months, never swapped**; five names on the standpipe rota; four hundred and forty-three years; nineteen names on the roll and eight outside their districts; two hundred and forty residents; ninety-one entries in the register of contested closures.

### 4. Every name in the volume, and whether it acquired a first name

A single table, in three blocks:

- **Docketford institutions with hands** — the reviewing officer, the seal-stair clerk, the clerk of petition 487-GS-19, the Glass Stair counters and docket clerks, the district officer who read the answer, the production clerk at the door, the countersignature desk clerks, the Mill Ward counter clerks, the Fetter Bend sub-table clerks, the county intake staff, the hall. **None of these acquires a first name anywhere in Volume 01, and the report must prove it.**
- **Ordinary-world people with names** — the ward, the offices that matter to them, the candidates, the women at two landings that must never be merged, and **the man at 4 Lantern Walk, who is `VANE, H.` and has no first name in any register in Docketford and never acquires one**, and **the man of Alder Row, who has no name on the page at all and does not get one**, and the man who wrote the ten minutes of bad slow hand on the ward's delivery book, who is not a mystery and does not become one.
- **The two names that went** — `VANE, H.` and **Hiram Cray** — with what each was, how long it had been arriving, on which day each went, and the standing prohibition on restoring, repeating, noticing or resolving either.

### 5. The short list of things Volume 02 must not contradict

A numbered list, hard-edged, at most about twenty items, each one a sentence a writer could break by accident. It is the operative hand-off and it must be the shortest and the bluntest part of the report. It must include at minimum: the two fixed numbers; the six instruments; the unamended Day 14 roll entry; the answer's date and the hearing date as two documents and the receipt that was never given; the twenty-one and the three absences; the two tide tables; the two forty-ones and the refusal to fuse them; **eleven** spent on exactly four things; all years being Concord years or a length of service; the class of persons who may object being eight and never nine; Ada Rusk not being a branch; a separate branch not being a pardon; **no custodian bought**; Cobb Rennie's pension uncured; Ada's wage unfunded; the crossing closed to loads and the hold not making it safe; the brass tab **shut and inherited shut**; the destroyed continuity statement unraised, unconfessed, unpriced; the four hundred and forty-three-year-old standard untouched; and the form maintainer's name **not looked up**.

### 6. A short plain assessment

One page, no more, in prose, answering: what the volume actually did, what its central sentence turned out to be, what is genuinely better about the world at Chapter 50 than at Chapter 1, and what is precisely worse. Then a paragraph on **what a reviewer of this volume should look for hardest in the next one** — not praise and not criticism, but the four or five places where Volume 02 could quietly undo Volume 01.

## The Standard

The report is an audit document and it must be **checkable**. Every claim carries a chapter and, where the claim is a number or a phrase, enough of the wording that a reader can find it in a minute. If the report cannot be verified against the prose, it is worthless, and a soft sentence in it will be quoted in Volume 09 as canon.

Be exact about the difference between **what the volume said** and **what the volume decided**. This volume is built on the gap between those two things, and a report that blurs them has described a different book.

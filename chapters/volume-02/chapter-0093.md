# Chapter 93 — Nine

The correction came down on the Monday morning of the eighth day of the fourth month of the Concord Year 488 and it was on the counter at the count room at the Glass Stair by twenty past eight and it was not in an envelope and it was dated the eighth, and the woman of about thirty with a slate was not the person who gave it to him.

A strip had come down on the Tuesday saying the line was closed. What came down on the Monday was the reason a line had to be closed, and it was four sheets, and it had a schedule of premises attached to it, and a date, and a reason in a box the size of a hand.

**COUNT CORRECTION. PROVINCIAL COUNTING OFFICE. Docketford district, season of 489. The schedule of premises attached. Made the eighth day of the fourth month of the Concord Year 488. Attested: Oress Kade, Warden of the Census Guard. Reason: 9.**

Nine.

He read the reason three times and then went and stood about eleven feet off with his back to a wall, because the first thing he had to do was not to say anything, and the second thing he had to do was to work out whether nine was the right figure, and it took him about ninety seconds and it was the right figure, and being right about it is not a thing anybody in this building has ever been able to put anywhere.

**It restores nothing and it takes their water and it is correct, and there is not one person in this county who is going to be able to make it anything else, and it took four sheets and ninety seconds to find that out and about nine seconds to accept it.**

---

Here is what the four sheets said, and the arithmetic in them is sound and the drafting is in the form's own voice and there is nothing in them that is anybody's fault.

The district's book of premises of record carries **nine premises** against a settlement, entered in the third column against the season 471, **struck in the fourth column with a clerk's stroke and no reason, and the fourth column is headed *struck, or not struck* and there is no fifth column in that book and there never has been one.** A line in an arithmetic in the Water Office of Docketford district has been authorised against a head named for that settlement's tank since the Concord Year 478, and the head cannot be married to a place, because the place is struck, and the Office does not examine the basis of a quantity.

The Office has two things it may do with a line that will not close. It may **close the line**, in which case the quantity returns to the district's main and the premises stay struck and the settlement remains not a place. Or it may **reinstate the premises**, in which case the settlement becomes a settlement of record with nine premises against it, the quantity is drawn against a place, and the count of the district carries nine where it carried none.

It has elected the second, and the reason in the box is **9**, and the reason is the figure of premises reinstated, and the reason is the right figure, and the reason is about doors.

**Nothing in those four sheets is about water. Not the head, not the thousand and ninety, not the hundred and ninety gallons a day that nobody has ever accounted for. A person reading the whole of this instrument in four years' time would learn that nine buildings came back into a list and would learn nothing whatever about where the water went or whether anybody had any.**

---

"Do you have any view," said the counting clerk at the second counter, from behind it, to nobody in particular.

"I have a view that the reason is a figure of the wrong thing."

"**Then you have a view that is correct and the form is going to say nine in the box, and it is the same nine that is in the schedule attached, and the two of them agree, and there is nobody in this county who is going to object to a reason that agrees with itself.**"

"Do they."

"**I have not asked, and I will not, and I will keep what I think a reason is for to myself, because I have been at this counter nine years and the answer to that question is the form, and the form says nine, and a building two hundred miles up a river decided that in about four minutes on the Tuesday and I have not seen the four minutes and I am not going to be told about them.**"

"Who attests it."

"She did," said the counting clerk. "**On the eighth, in the morning, in about a minute, and she did not go to the place, and she is not going to, and it is not a thing anybody is going to ask her about, and I would like that said out loud at a counter one time, that she did not go and that nobody is going to ask.**"

---

**The figure was right. He proved it to himself in a room with a bulb on a flex on the Monday afternoon and he did it in about eleven minutes and then he sat on the edge of the table for a quarter of an hour doing nothing at all, which he has not done in this building in nine years.**

The nine premises are the nine. He has the settlement book and the third column and the fourth column, and the fourth column has one word in it in nine cells and the word is *struck*, and the third column says 471 in nine cells, and the fourth and the third are in the same ink in the same hand, and a stroke is the only decision anybody has ever made about this place and nobody wrote down what it was a decision about.

The count for the season of 489 was certified on the twenty-eighth of the third month with **no figure of premises of record upon it at all**, for the first time in the record of this county, and the reason there is no figure is on the second line of that certification, and it is a sentence of arithmetic, and the district office that made the figures is two miles down the hill from the room he was sitting in.

And from the moment this correction is made, Docketford district carries nine premises of record again, in a settlement that nobody has looked at, in a season in which nobody looked at anything, and the figure is correct, and the district is still short, and the report of the shortage is still the ten settlements named on a schedule printed in the last week of the second month with *no* and *yes* against all ten of them, and **none of that is amended by this and none of it should be.**

He got up and went and got the district's own book of premises of record off the shelf, which is bound in canvas with brass corners and has no name on the spine and has been on that shelf since the Concord Year 348.

Then he ruled nine lines into it in his own hand, in the third column with the season, and in the fourth column nine times the word **not struck**, and he put the day and the season against the whole of it at the foot, and his name, because he is the officer of the record of the district returns and that is what the book is the record of.

He looked at it for a while.

**There is no column in that book for why, and there never has been one, and he had just put his own name under nine houses coming back into a list, and the reason was on four sheets in a building two hundred miles up a river and it was a figure, and the figure was right.**

He did not write anything else. He did not write *reinstated on a correction dated the eighth of the fourth month* under the nine, because a line under an entry in that book is a different kind of act and the book does not have a place for it. That is not an oversight. **It is the third time this year that the whole difficulty has been the absence of a place to write a true sentence, and the third time is not going to be different from the first two, and the reason he has started noticing that is that he has run out of paper.**

---

**He went up the track on the Tuesday morning of the ninth day at about seven and got to the top of the climb at ten past eight, and Jos Harker was on the wheel at the sluice and the wheel would not turn more than a quarter of the way and stopped with a sound in it like somebody knocking.**

"They are going to shut it," said Jos Harker.

"Who is going to shut it."

"**Nobody. That is what I am telling you. There is nobody. There is no work here, there is no premises, there is nobody whose duty it is, and the water in that beck is going to come down the yard on a Tuesday and take the cart house and the pump house with it, and if the screw goes the frame goes, and if the frame goes there is nothing at all between this yard and about nine feet of water.**"

He said it in the voice of a man reporting on a thing he has been working on for nine years, and he did not raise it, and he did not ask Owen Rusk for anything, and that was the correct arrangement and it was the arrangement Owen Rusk had come to hear.

"**You are the officer of the record of the district returns and there is a correction on a counter with nine on it and I do not know what nine is and I have not asked and I am not going to, and what I want to say to you is a thing I have said to nobody and I am saying it to you because you are the first person from a building that has stood at this wheel.**"

"Say it."

"**I have been keeping this place on my own back for nine years and I have never once been able to say so to anybody, because the moment I say it out loud it is a claim on something and a claim is a thing you put in a form, and there is no form in this county for a man saying that a sluice needs mending, and I would like it recorded that there is no form.**"

"It is recorded."

"**Then I have said it, and that is the end of it, and I have work to do, and I would like you to stand about four feet further off because the wheel comes off the screw when it binds.**"

Owen Rusk stood about four feet further off.

**And the thing that has to be said plainly about the ninth day of the fourth month of the Concord Year 488, because the whole of this has been walking toward it since the twenty-eighth of the third month, and it comes down to this: the water is real, the quantity is authorised, the ledger is the district's, the district office is the office that computed the district's allocation, and Owen Rusk is the officer of the record of the returns that carry the figure, and the figure is nine, and the figure is correct, and the sluice has no premises against it, and a sluice is a work, and the count is a count of works maintained, and there is no work in this county that anybody is obliged to maintain, because nobody has ever written down that there is one.**

On the way down he stopped at the wall of the yard and looked at the tank for about two minutes, and there was a woman of eighty-four sitting in the one chair in the yard under a blanket in the sun on the ninth day of the fourth month, and she looked at him and did not speak, and he did not ask her anything, and he has not written down in any book that he did not ask her anything, because it is not his book to write that in, and because the woman of eighty-four asked him nothing and the day she asks him something is the day there is a record, and he is not the person who will make it.

---

**Junia Pell was at the rail at the second counter at about four on the Tuesday afternoon of the ninth day and she had already read it, and she said the thing he had spent two days not saying, which is that he was going to go looking for a way round it and there was not one, and she said it before he had got to the counter, which she has done four times this year.**

"You may not examine the schedule of settlements to be reconciled, and this is not that. You may examine arithmetic, procedure, and the currency of any paper a reconciliation rests upon, and this is all three, and it is sound in all three, and I have been in this building three months and a fortnight and I would like you to sit down."

"I have not said anything."

"You have not said anything and you have got the expression of a man going through a list of doors in his own head, and I have watched you do it about nine times this year, and the list has never once had a door in it and I have got tired of watching." She put the copy down and squared it. "**Read me the appeal.**"

"The appeal lies to the Glass Stair on application, seven days from the stone, a person of the district, a current local act performed by the settlement, heard before an officer of the count with no district in the matter, and the requester named on a public file."

"**Yes, and there is a stone about ninety miles up this river in a case on a handcart and the season is over and the Guard is closing, and if you go and find that stone you will find a man who will be very pleased to hear from you and it will cost this office about a season and the thing you want to say to that man is that the head in the ledger has no address, and you cannot put that to him, because the reason box on his instrument says nine and the head is nowhere on the four sheets, and a warden who will strike her own schedule about a word is not going to strike her own attestation about a line she cannot see.**"

"That is not a refusal."

"**It is not a refusal, it is the route, and the route is shut, and I have just watched you go and find it shut, and I would like to say before you start on the other two things that they are also shut and for the same reason and I do not want to spend an hour on the same sentence three times.**"

"Then tell me the sentence once and properly."

"**The sentence is that the reason in the box is a figure and the figure is correct and the figure is about doors, and the count is a count of draws, premises of record and works maintained, and the thing you want to say is about a draw, and there is no column on that instrument for a draw, and there is no appeal in this county against the absence of a column, and the absence of a column is not a decision anybody has taken and there is nobody to sue for it and there is nobody to be angry at and I have watched you look for somebody to be angry at for three months.**" She picked the copy up. "And you are not going to find one, because the man who wrote the head in 478 is dead, and the man who typed the nine out of the district's own book in 486 is at a desk in a building in Concord Hall and has never been asked anything and does not know that nine means anything, and the woman who attested it is a hundred and sixty miles away and suspended, and there is nobody in that chain of hands who chose anything. **A person can be wrong in a form. There is a woman in this county who says that about four times a season and she is right and it is the reason you cannot get at any of it.**"

"And the day of this year," said Owen Rusk, "when you told me to send the paper anyway. Was that the same as this."

"No," said Junia Pell. "**That was about a form that could be filled. This one cannot. I told you in the spring to send it anyway and I meant it then, and I mean it now, and it is the same sentence, and you are going to send something, and nothing I say will stop you and I am not trying to, and the thing I am going to say is that whatever you send is going to be a document about a document and nobody in this county has ever read one, and I have said that to you before this year and I am saying it again and I am not going to be thanked for it, so put that on the same page as everything else you have got this season.**"

"That is four times this year."

"**It is four times this year and it will be four times next year and I would like you to notice that the register of it is in my hand and not in yours, because about four years from now somebody is going to read one file in this building with two names on it and mine is going to be the one doing the arithmetic and yours is going to be the one that signed, and I would like the arithmetic to be legible.**"

She put the copy down on the counter and he had it and it was four sheets and he did not leave it on the rail.

And it was the fourth document in a district file in one season with a name at the foot of it that was not a form he had filled in: the reason column granted in pencil on the ninth of the first month, the statement concerning the district of Lower Lock on the fourth of the third month, the ward's own paper of eight refusals and three acceptances on the twenty-first of the third month, and the preparation line of the schedule of the settlements not reconciled on the twenty-eighth of the third month. The correction is not in that district file and the four sheets are not in it and he has not sent them up the stairs, and there is a clerk at the second counter who could have carried them up in about ninety seconds and did not offer.

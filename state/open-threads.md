# Open Threads

This file is author-facing planning. It is not a list of facts already known by the opening cast.

## Central Mystery

- Can Form Zero be completed without a single person answering for everyone else?
- What did the Registry conceal about the original distributed rule, the Blanking, and the Assumption Clause?
- How did Owen’s eight-person infancy sponsorship become a distributed root claim, and what does it cost him to use it?
- How can a bounded completeness procedure protect a real world while honestly recording the Free Counters and other branches it cannot see?
- What obligations does the new civic order owe people whose claims remain unresolved after activation, and how do they use appeals without letting either Lound’s old machinery or Owen’s Custodian role reclaim them?

## Immediate Volume 1 Promises

- Lower Lock and the Ninth Weir need a lawful local return before a closure order becomes irreversible.
- Halden Pike’s stability order must be challenged through evidence and appeals, not a hidden power.
- Owen and Nell must work together while retaining their professional disagreement.
- Ada must be allowed to negotiate Lower Lock’s future rather than becoming Owen’s protected ward.
- Any crossing hold preserves a legal appeal window, not the physical safety or usability of the Ninth Weir.

## Relationship and Character Payoffs

- Owen’s habit of concealing information will eventually rupture trust with Nell; the rupture must be earned through a real choice, not foreshadowed as present knowledge.
- Junia Pell must resist Owen early, coordinate independent clerks in Volume 9, and end in an institutional role that does not depend on him.
- Bettis Marr’s costly correction belongs to Volume 9: a dissenting household leaves the Commons without losing access to shared stores.

## Long-Term Payoffs

- Vespera’s emergency mandate expires at Civic Zero at the end of C.Y. 500.
- Verity Lound’s Assumption Plan is the final political opponent; Sallow’s market, Rive’s secrecy, and Kade’s suspension doctrine are earlier stages of the same authority conflict.
- Lound’s institutional proxy authority and Owen’s community-selected transmission role must remain distinct at the climax.
- The final form must be completed by plural voluntary returns within a bounded, public network, with irreversible losses acknowledged and no new cosmic enemy.
